SOCIAL VIDEO KIT
One 16:9 animation, every social format. Beat-synced, loop-perfect.

Open the panel: 3D Viewport, press N, then pick the Social Video tab. Every section has a ? button with a short how-to.

01 Vertical Reframe

  1. Finish your animation in 16:9 as usual.
  2. Click 9:16 (or 4:5, 1:1). A copy of the scene is made with every camera lens re-fitted. Your original is never touched. Switch between versions with the buttons at the top of the panel.
  3. Set Subject to your product (an object or a whole collection) and click Auto-Follow Subject. Every frame is reframed so the product stays inside the frame. Tick Fill frame to also zoom in on small subjects.
  4. Click All Platforms to see the area that stays clear of the buttons and captions on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts at once, or pick one platform. Auto-Follow then centres the product in that safe area.
  5. Render All Formats exports the original and every version as H.264 MP4 (with your music) to the Export Folder.
Not happy with Auto-Follow? Click X to restore the original lens, change settings, run it again. It never stacks.

02 Camera Guides

Drawn in the viewport in camera view only. They never appear in your renders.

  • Format badge: the current frame format and size.
  • Lime box: the platform safe area. Keep text and product inside it.
  • Lime dot: pulses on every beat marker, so you can feel the edit while scrubbing.

Toggle them with Camera Guides. The eye button jumps to camera view.

03 Beat Sync

  1. Detect Beats: pick an mp3 or wav. A marker lands on every beat and the track is added as a sound strip. If the tempo reads half or double, use Tempo ½ / Tempo x2.
  2. No audio yet? Beat Grid makes markers from a BPM you choose.
  3. Select objects, then Squash, Hop, Light or Glow keys a pulse on every beat. Fine-tune it in the redo panel (bottom-left): every N beats, amount, decay, stagger.
  4. Select two or more cameras, then Camera Cuts on Beats cuts between them in time with the music.

04 Loop Doctor

  1. Set the frame range to exactly one loop.
  2. Check Loop lists every animation that doesn't end where it starts, and any video texture that will freeze.
  3. Fix Loop closes them. A full 360° spin already counts as closed.

Works on the selected objects, or on everything when nothing is selected.

05 Wedge Batch

  1. Right-click any number field and choose Copy Full Data Path.
  2. Click Wedge Render. The path is pasted for you. Choose From, To and Steps.
  3. You get one still per value plus a labelled contact sheet. Open Last Contact Sheet shows it.

06 Where files go

Everything is written to the Export Folder shown in the panel. The default is a folder called Social Video Exports next to your .blend file. If the file hasn't been saved yet, it goes to Videos/Social Video Exports in your home folder instead. You can change it at any time; the folder icon opens it.

  • Renders/: MP4s named like MyScene_9x16.mp4
  • Wedges/: one folder per wedge with the stills, wedge_sheet.png and the values
